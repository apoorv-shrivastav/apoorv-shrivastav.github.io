The professional side of this site is about evals, retrieval and agents. This side is for everything else.

I grew up in India and moved to the Bay Area in December 2021.

My idea of a good weekend is pretty simple. Get in the car with nowhere in particular to be, find a trail I haven't done before, and end the day somewhere with good music and good food. If it turns into a road trip or a night of camping, even better.

I'm into most things that get me outside and moving, especially anything that counts as an adventure sport. I play cricket, football and table tennis whenever I get the chance. This winter I'm learning to snowboard.

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm probably watching Formula 1. I watch every race. Lately I've also been listening to a lot of podcasts about physical AI.

This is where I'll write about all of it: drives, trails, races, slopes, and whatever else I'm thinking about when I'm not building things. Mostly short notes.

Right now, I'm counting down to snowboarding season.
